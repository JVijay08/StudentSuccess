STUDENTSUCCESS

A student planner for assignments and courses.

Organize what to work on today and how your courses fit together, from high school and dual enrollment to college terms.

Home

Turn assignments into clear next steps

Create tasks with deadlines and work estimates. Break larger projects into subtasks or work blocks, reschedule planned starts, and keep completed work separate. Reuse tasks as templates or select several tasks for cleanup.

The recommended queue uses explained rules: work in progress, overdue deadlines, missed planned starts, and upcoming deadlines. Effort, challenge, interest, and recorded start history help order tasks within those groups. These are planning suggestions, not grades or predictions.

Plan high-school, dual-enrollment, and college courses

Browse and compare available high-school course references, then build a four-year plan. Add college courses alongside high-school courses for dual enrollment. In the college preset, organize courses by term, track entered credits and study hours, and choose different colleges for different courses.

Course catalogs and workload estimates are reference information. Coverage varies by source. Confirm availability, admission requirements, and transfer credit with your school or college.

Repeat tasks through an end date

Choose daily, weekly, every-two-week, or monthly repeats, a start date, and an inclusive end date. Each occurrence uses your chosen due time, with 11:59 PM as the default. You can edit or complete the resulting tasks individually.

Bring deadlines to Google Calendar

Open a prefilled deadline event to review and save in Google Calendar, or download active deadlines as an ICS file for bulk import. These are copies: later changes in StudentSuccess do not automatically sync to Google.

Frequently asked questions

Can I try StudentSuccess before creating an account?

Yes. The interactive tutorial creates a separate practice workspace and guides you through the site. Exiting the tutorial deletes that practice workspace.

Do I need an email address or real name?

No. Accounts use a username and password. Use real coursework and tasks, but leave out full names, student numbers, and other sensitive details. Settings includes data export and account deletion.

Can I plan courses at more than one college?

Yes. Each college course can have its own institution, including dual-enrollment courses in the high-school preset.

Can I customize accessibility settings?

Settings includes text size, contrast, spacing, and reduced motion. Keyboard navigation and browser zoom remain available.
