StudentSuccess helps you organize academic work. It is an independent project operated from Georgia, United States, and is not affiliated with your school or college.

Using your account

You must be at least 13 to create an account. If you are a minor, use the service with a parent or guardian's permission where required. Use a nickname-style username and keep your password safe. There is currently no email password reset, so save your credentials securely.

You are responsible for the content you enter. Use your actual courses and everyday tasks, but leave out personal identifiers, private records, and other people's personal information. Do not misuse accounts, attempt unauthorized access, upload harmful content, or bypass service limits.

Your content and choices

Your content remains yours. You allow StudentSuccess to store and process it to provide the planner and the features you request. AI is optional and requires an explicit sharing acknowledgment. The privacy policy explains processing; it is not a request for blanket consent to unrelated uses.

Planning guidance has limits

Task rankings, workload estimates, catalog references, and AI suggestions are planning aids. They are not official academic, admissions, graduation, medical, or financial advice. Verify course details and requirements with your institution. AI can be inaccurate or omit requirements; review suggestions before saving or using them. No grades, outcomes, or uninterrupted availability are guaranteed.

Availability and closing an account

This project may change, limit, or discontinue features. Keep an export of important work. Access may be restricted to address abuse, security issues, or legal obligations. You can stop using the service and delete your account. Nothing here removes rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.
