StudentSuccess aims to make planning usable with a keyboard, screen readers, and different display preferences. This is an ongoing effort, not a claim of full WCAG certification.

Available controls

  • Use the Skip to main content link at the start of each page.
  • Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls, Enter to follow links, and Enter or Space to open disclosure menus. Escape closes a task's options menu.
  • Settings offers larger text, dark and high-contrast themes, reduced motion, and layout preferences.
  • Forms have labels and the interface uses visible text alongside important status colors. Decorative icons are hidden from assistive technology; meaningful images need descriptive alternatives.

We check representative pages for contrast, labeled controls, responsive layout, and keyboard operation. Automated checks cannot establish that every interaction works for every assistive technology.

Help with an accessibility barrier

A dedicated contact channel is still being set up. When one is available, a useful report will include the page, the control, and your browser or assistive technology, without private planner contents.
