Add one deadline

Open a task's three-dot menu or detail page and choose Add deadline to Google Calendar. Review the title, date, and time in Google, choose your calendar, then save.

The event is a one-minute deadline marker, not the time you plan to work. Clicking the link sends the task title and deadline to Google. StudentSuccess does not receive your Google password or access your calendar.

Copies, not automatic sync

Changing, rescheduling, completing, or deleting a task here does not change its Google Calendar event. Update or remove that copy in Google Calendar yourself. Importing or adding the same deadline again can create duplicates; check the destination calendar first.
