Plan your actual courses and tasks.

Course names, college selections, deadlines, and everyday tasks are welcome. Leave out full names, student IDs, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, passwords, and private records.

How codes, saved plans, and the demo work
  • A private code replaces signup details. Keep it safe: anyone with it can open your planner, and a lost code can’t be recovered.
  • Code-based plans are saved on our server. They are not anonymous or encrypted by the code. You can export or delete them in Settings.
  • Just looking? The demo comes with sample tasks and courses. You don’t need to enter anything.
