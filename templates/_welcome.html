How codes, saved plans, and the demo work
  • A private code replaces signup details. Keep it safe: anyone with it can open your planner, and a lost code can’t be recovered.
  • Code-based plans are saved on our server. They are not anonymous or encrypted by the code. You can export or delete them in Settings.
  • Just looking? The fictional demo comes with sample tasks and courses. You don’t need to enter anything.
