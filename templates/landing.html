About the Developer

JVJayesh Vijay

Independent Student Developer

StudentSuccess grew from a simple challenge: helping students decide what to work on next and why. Jayesh independently designed, built, and launched the platform to make academic planning more manageable and personalized.

Built with Python, Flask, JavaScript, and PostgreSQL, StudentSuccess helps students prioritize assignments, organize coursework, and adapt their schedules. User feedback has guided ongoing improvements to usability, task planning, and personalized recommendations.
