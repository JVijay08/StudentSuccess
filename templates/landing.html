About the Developer

JVJayesh Vijay

Designed, developed, and launched independently.

StudentSuccess was independently designed, developed, and launched by Jayesh Vijay, a high school student passionate about computer science and solving real-world problems.

Built using Python, Flask, JavaScript, and PostgreSQL, the platform helps students make informed decisions about academic priorities, manage coursework, and adapt their schedules. User feedback guided ongoing improvements to usability, task planning, and personalized recommendations.
