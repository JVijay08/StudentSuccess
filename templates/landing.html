Set it up for your life.

Start small. Your planner can grow with your semester.

  1. Make it yours

    Create a username and password, then choose your education setting and planning preferences. No email address or real name needed.

  2. Put the work on paper

    Add courses and assignments, set deadlines, and break bigger projects into manageable steps.

  3. Take the next step

    Open Today, choose a task, and begin. Adjust your plan when things change.

Want to explore first?

The interactive tutorial walks you through a separate sample planner. The free server may take up to a minute to wake.

A few things to know.

Do I have to use AI?

No. You can create tasks, plan courses, and use the task recommendations without AI. AI drafting is optional and requires your review before anything is saved.

Can I use it for both high school and college?

Yes. High-school planning includes dual enrollment. College planning supports terms, credits, and courses at multiple institutions.

What happens to my information?

Your account saves your planner so you can return on another device. Use a nickname and keep sensitive personal information out of tasks. You can export your data or delete your account in Settings. Read the features and FAQ, or review the privacy links below.

StudentSuccess was independently designed, developed, and launched by Jayesh Vijay, a high school student passionate about computer science and solving real-world problems.
